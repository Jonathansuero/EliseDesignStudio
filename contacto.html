FAQ

      
Before we start

    
How do we get started?
Write to us through the form, by email or on Instagram. We reply in under 30 minutes and schedule a 15-minute Zoom consultation to learn about your project and set next steps.
Where do you work?
Our main service area is all of Florida: Miami, Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach, Tampa, Orlando and many more cities. We also serve other U.S. states with certain restrictions: design, drawings and renders are delivered remotely, and construction or installation are reviewed depending on the state and scope.
Can I hire just one part of the process?
Yes. You can hire a single service, like 3D renders or material selection, or the full project including construction.
How is a project quoted?
After the initial consultation we prepare a proposal based on scope, spaces and level of finish. Projects with construction include a detailed estimate.
How long does a full design take?
It depends on size. A single room can be resolved in a few weeks; a whole home with construction takes several months. You'll get a schedule with the proposal.
Are meetings in Spanish or English?
In whichever you prefer. The whole team works in both.
Do you work with my contractor?
Yes. We deliver clear drawings and specifications and can visit the site to make sure the design is built as approved.
