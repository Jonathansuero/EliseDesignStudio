The studio

Design through an architect's eye

Elise Design Studio is the practice of Elisa Benzan, interior designer and architect. We work all across Florida with one team for design, renders, millwork and construction.

From the plan to the last piece

My training in architecture taught me to think about structure, light and proportion. Interior design taught me to think about the people who live inside.

Alongside design, I prepare estimates for remodeling and ground-up construction projects. That experience keeps my design grounded: I know what each decision costs and how it gets built on site.

My style is warm and calm: natural woods, earthy tones, indirect light and custom pieces. But every project ends up looking like the people who live in it, not like me.

Elisa Benzan

What guides us

Three principles in every project

  • Listen first

    Every project starts by understanding how you live, what bothers you about your space and how you want to feel when you get home.

  • Design with intention

    Nothing is decorative by accident. Light, materials and every dimension answer to a real use.

  • Build with precision

    We know construction and the numbers. We design what can be built, with clear estimates and no surprises.
