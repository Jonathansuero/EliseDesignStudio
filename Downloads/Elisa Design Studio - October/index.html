Hi, I'm Elisa Benzan

Interior designer and architect. I founded Elise Design Studio to guide every project from start to finish, pairing design vision with hands-on construction and estimating experience.

Listen first, design with intention and build with precision. That's how warm, timeless spaces built to last come together.

How we work

A clear process, no surprises

  2. 01

    15-minute Zoom consultation

    We start with a short video call: we learn about your space, your style and your budget, and define the scope together.

  3. 02

    Concept and moodboard

    We propose the direction: layout, palette, materials and references, so you can see where we're headed.

  4. 03

    Plans, renders and estimate

    We develop drawings and 3D renders and prepare a detailed estimate so you decide with full information.

  5. 04

    Build and handover

    We coordinate purchasing, millwork, construction and installation until your space is ready to live in.
